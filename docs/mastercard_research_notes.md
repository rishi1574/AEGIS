# Mastercard Research Notes

- **The Problem:** Rule-based systems and static ML models suffer from "Concept Drift" as fraud evolves faster than models are retrained.
- **The GenAI Threat:** Fraud is now cheaper and automated. E.g., Agentic Hijacking can perform 1000s of transactions a second.
- **Mastercard's Vision:** Needs a self-healing system. We achieve this by letting an AI Red Team generate the *future* data that the Blue Team trains on.
